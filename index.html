Why this exists

From literature spreadsheet to living research infrastructure.

This portal organizes literature relevant to multimodal physiological computing and the ARL human decision-making research program. The long-term goal is a transparent, versioned resource that supports dataset discovery, systematic review, experimental design, and gap analysis.
