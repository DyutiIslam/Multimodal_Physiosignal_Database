03 · Evidence gap challenge

We tried to break the novelty claim

Targeted searches were designed to invalidate candidate ARL gaps. Most individual features were successfully challenged.

STRONGEST SURVIVING WORKING GAP

Group ≥3 + neural and peripheral physiology + eye tracking + audio/video + repeated decisions + explicit confidence + deception + event-level synchronization

No match was found among the audited comparators and targeted gap-challenge results. This is a working evidence-gap hypothesis, not a claim that ARL is definitively the first such study.

About

A literature resource built to test a research gap

Scope. Multimodal physiological datasets and experimental studies relevant to synchronized human decision-making.

Ownership. External datasets and publications belong to their respective authors, institutions and publishers. Their inclusion here does not imply ARL authorship, sponsorship or ownership.

Status. This is a curated evidence resource with a 50-study comparator audit and targeted gap-challenge layer. It should not yet be described as a completed systematic review.

Interpretation. “Not found” means not found within the evidence reviewed to date; it does not prove global absence from the literature.
